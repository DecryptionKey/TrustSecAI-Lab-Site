Research Focus

Adversarial Robustness

Design and evaluate defense mechanisms to protect AI systems (e.g., fraud detection, risk scoring, intrusion detection) from adversarial attacks.

IoT Privacy in Health

Enhance privacy in health-centric IoT ecosystems using generative models (e.g., diffusion) for anonymization and differential privacy for rigorous protections.

LLM Hallucination & Privacy

Verify retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines and apply secure multi-party computation (MPC) to reduce leakage and mitigate hallucinations.
