Why This Research Matters

AI powers decision-making across sectors by processing vast, high-velocity data streams. That influence makes reliability and trust a security issue as much as a technical one.

TrustSec AI Lab Works to Keep Systems

  • Reliable when facing uncertainty, pressure, or attack.
  • Robust when deployed in interconnected and high-consequence environments.
  • Worthy of trust when they affect people, institutions, and mission-critical operations.

Potential Impact on Education

  • Teach defense strategies against adversarial ML attacks on AI-powered systems (e.g., healthcare informatics, cyber-physical systems, credit scoring).
  • Assess vulnerabilities in AI models used in CEHC-affiliated research.
  • Equip students with DL skills to design, evaluate, and deploy robust AI.
  • Conduct behavioral analytics with industry partners to detect and mitigate fraud.
  • Foster collaborations with private industry specializing in cybersecurity and AI.
